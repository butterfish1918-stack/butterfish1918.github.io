Instrument guide

Play the circle

Heliocentric is deliberately non-linear: each concentric ring is a sound, and each slice around the ring is one of 16 rhythmic steps.

1
Build a patternTap a ring segment to switch that step on or off. The ring you touch becomes the selected instrument.
2
Shape the systemDrag inward or outward on the outer labelled arcs to change tempo, volume, elasticity, entropy, morph, root and feedback.
3
Shape one soundSelect a track, then drag its inner parameter arcs to alter FM ratio, timbre, detune, envelope, pitch motion, filter, probability and diffusion.
✦
GenerateUse Generate for a complete new system. Clear removes all active steps without resetting the sound design.
